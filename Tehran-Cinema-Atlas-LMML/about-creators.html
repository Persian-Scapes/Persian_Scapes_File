About the creator

The project team

Tehran Cinema Atlas combines cinema research, location evidence, information modelling, narrative design and front-end development.

SA

Research · information modelling · interaction design · development

Saman Abdolrashidi

Master’s student in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna. The project explores how Iranian films can be connected to visitable Tehran locations through active documents, adaptive narratives and deliberate visual systems.

Course context

Information Modeling and Web Technologies

The project was developed for the 2025–26 LMML assignment under Professor Fabio Vitali. It treats the city as a distributed museum: locations are the items, narratives are the curated paths, and the web interface is both a planning tool and an on-site companion.
