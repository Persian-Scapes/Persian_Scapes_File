# Image and media checklist

Before public deployment, record for every poster, film still and present-day photograph:

- creator or photographer
- title or description
- original source URL or archive
- date accessed
- copyright owner
- licence or legal basis for use
- required credit line
- whether cropping, colour treatment or compression is permitted

Also:

- replace any image that cannot be legally published
- add alt text that describes the useful visual evidence
- keep the film still and present-day photograph clearly labelled
- verify that the location comparison does not imply certainty beyond the research evidence
- add only short, licensed dialogue clips and record their rights information
