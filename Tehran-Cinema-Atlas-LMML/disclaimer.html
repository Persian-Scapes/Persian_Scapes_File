Academic purpose

The purpose of this website is to explore typographic and layout styles for specialized tourist visits as an end-of-course project for the Information Modeling and Web Technologies course of the Master’s Degree in Digital Humanities and Digital Knowledge at the University of Bologna, under the supervision of Professor Fabio Vitali.

Selection and original locations

The documents, descriptions and images in this prototype were selected from the research material supplied with the Tehran Cinema Atlas project. Their publication here is not intended to replace original films, archives, websites, museums or source records. The source URLs available in the dataset are shown on the relevant location and film pages.

Visiting real locations

Do not enter private property, obstruct entrances, photograph where prohibited, or recreate unsafe camera positions. Check current opening hours, fees, security rules and accessibility. Several locations are active institutions or protected heritage sites.

Accuracy and field verification

Coordinates, names and identification claims are research aids. The site explicitly marks camera orientation as requiring field verification because the supplied data does not include dependable bearings. Buildings and access conditions may have changed.

External map services

Outbound map and direction links require an internet connection and are governed by the relevant service provider. The core catalogue, images, metadata and offline SVG map remain readable without them.
